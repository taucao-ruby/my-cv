IMPACT & ENGINEERING DECISIONS

ZERO DOUBLE-BOOKING, BY CONSTRUCTION

Outcome: revenue & guest trust protected even under concurrent load

Made a PostgreSQL EXCLUDE overlap constraint the source of truth rather than application-level locks — so correctness survives app bugs, retries and horizontal scaling, not just the happy path. Reinforced with FOR UPDATE locking, optimistic version checks and TOCTOU-safe restore / refund / cancellation paths, verified by concurrency stress tests. Correctness is the product here, so it earns the rigor.

IDEMPOTENT DUAL PAYMENTS (STRIPE + MoMo)

Outcome: no double-charges · safe to retry · survives restart & scale-out

Replaced an in-memory idempotency guard with a durable UNIQUE ledger (stripe_refund_events, momo_webhook_events), closing the TOCTOU window and cutting ~500 lines of fragile state. Added MoMo e-wallet with IPN sign/verify — a deliberate product call to fit how Vietnamese guests actually pay, not just the default Stripe path.
